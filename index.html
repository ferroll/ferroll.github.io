About the game

Ferroll is a physics-driven maze game built for iPhone and iPad. You never tap to move — you tilt your device, and a steel ball rolls with real momentum. Every wall you clip, every corner you take too fast, costs you time.

Collect the diamonds scattered through each labyrinth to unlock the exit door, then reach it before the timer runs out. Lava traps open and close on their own rhythm, and ghosts phase through corridors hunting for you. Learn the timing, or get caught.

🎯 42 story levels

Five visual worlds, each with its own hazards and pace. Rooms stack up as you progress.

♾️ Endless mode

Descend as deep as you can into a maze that never stops generating. One life, one run.

🏆 Game Center leaderboard

Your best endless score is submitted automatically. Compare it against players worldwide.

⭐ Three-star challenge

Stars depend on how fast you finish and how few times you die. Perfect runs set records.

👻 Traps and ghosts

Lava pits telegraph before they open, ghosts glow before they charge. Everything is learnable.

🌍 16 languages

Fully localized, including English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Japanese and Chinese.
