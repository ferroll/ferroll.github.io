About the game

Ferroll is a physics-driven maze game for iPhone, iPad and Android. You never tap to move — you tilt your device, and an iron ball rolls with real momentum. Every wall you clip, every corner you take too fast, costs you time.

Collect the gems scattered through each labyrinth to unlock the exit door, then reach it before the timer runs out. Traps open and close on their own rhythm, and ghosts patrol the corridors. Learn the timing, or get caught.

🎯 42 story levels

Five visual worlds, each with its own walls, ghosts, gems and traps. Mazes grow as you progress.

♾️ Endless mode

Descend floor after floor in a maze that never ends. Every gem adds 5 seconds and 50 points.

🏆 Global leaderboards

Your best endless run goes to Game Center on iPhone and iPad, and to Google Play Games on Android.

⭐ Three-star challenge

Stars depend on how fast you finish and how few times you die. Flawless runs set best times.

❤️ Lives that refill

Out of lives? You get one back every 20 minutes, up to three. New in version 1.5.

🌍 14 languages

Fully localized, including English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Japanese and Chinese.
